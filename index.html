01 About

AI that keeps working after the demo.

I’m a machine learning and software engineer who cares most about the part of AI that happens after the prototype: running models reliably, at scale, inside real products.

At BSI, a Swiss CRM software company, I own the company-wide AI platform — the LLM, speech, embedding and document services that our products and enterprise customers use in production. Before taking on that role, I built AI and data science components for the CRM platform and delivered machine learning projects for enterprise customers.

My work is equally rooted in research. Alongside industry, I’m the lead developer of the eHealth application for the ComBaCaL project at University Hospital Basel — work that has contributed to peer-reviewed trials published in Nature Medicine, The Lancet Global Health and BMC Medicine.

Off the clock

Endurance sports, aviation and drone videography. I received a recommendation in the Swiss SPHAIR pilot assessment.

03 Work

Selected work

Projects I can show in public — from clinical software in rural Lesotho to conversational AI.

FeaturedUniversity Hospital Basel · since 2023

ComBaCaL app

Clinical decision support that lets community health workers treat hypertension and diabetes in rural Lesotho

An offline-first tablet application built on the open-source Community Health Toolkit. It guides lay health workers through screening, diagnosis, starting and adjusting treatment, referral and follow-up — work usually reserved for clinicians — and manages a research cohort of more than 14,000 people across 103 villages, including electronic consent.

Role Lead developer. Started in a team of three student developers; today the sole developer in a team of medical researchers.

  • 103community health workers use the app in the field
  • 58%reached blood-pressure control with health-worker-led care, vs. 48% with facility-based care (Nature Medicine)
  • 95%of health workers are satisfied with the app (usability score 75.6)

Community Health Toolkit · Offline-first · Clinical decision support · eConsent

combacal.org ↗ Trial in Nature Medicine ↗ Usability study (preprint) ↗

Pedagogical conversational agent

A voice-based digital coach for corporate identity training in public administration

Master’s thesis in the UZH research project “Improving Public Service with Conversational A.I.”, run with the University of Public Administration and Finance Ludwigsburg and two German cities. I designed, built and evaluated a voice agent that coaches public servants over the phone and adapts the training to each employee: speech recognition, an LLM and speech synthesis working together in a live call — the same building blocks I now run in production.

Role Design, implementation and evaluation · Grade 5.5 / 6

BSc thesisUniversity of Zurich

Intersession app → InMind

A smartphone companion for psychotherapy between sessions

Patients spend most of their therapy outside the therapy room. With the UZH chair of Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy Research, I built the first prototype of an app that helps them apply and reflect on therapy content between sessions. It was presented at the SPR 2021 international conference and evolved into the InMind app, which UZH evaluated with psychotherapists in training in the EVALIS study.

Role Concept and first prototype · Grade 5.75 / 6

React Native · Mobile app · eHealth · Psychotherapy research
